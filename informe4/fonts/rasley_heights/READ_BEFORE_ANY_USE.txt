Rasley Heights is a high-contrast calligraphy script built around long, drawn-out swashes and broad capitals.

The hand behind it leans hard on the downstroke and lifts to a hairline on the way back up, the way a pointed pen does when the writer trusts the paper. Caps open with a wide loop and finish in a curl that reaches into the next letter. Lowercase moves more steadily, keeping the rhythm while the capitals carry the show.

It belongs on a hotel marquee, the cover plate of a wedding invitation, the brass nameplate of a building that wants to look older than it is.

Designed and created by Mans Greback.

The font is built with advanced OpenType functionality and guaranteed top-notch quality, containing stylistic and contextual alternates, ligatures and more automatic and manual features; all to give you full control and customizability.
It has extensive lingual support, covering all Latin-based languages, from North Europa to South Africa, from America to South-East Asia. It contains all characters and symbols you'll ever need, including all punctuation and numbers.
